Cristiano Ronaldo

About Cristiano

1985 — Present · The Greatest Footballer

Cristiano Ronaldo was born on 5 February 1985 in Madeira, Portugal. He is one of the greatest football players in history, known for his incredible work ethic, athleticism, and goal-scoring ability. He has won five Ballon d'Or awards and multiple UEFA Champions League titles. He has played for Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, and Al Nassr.

Key Events

  1. 2002 — Professional debut at Sporting CP
  2. 2003 — Joined Manchester United
  3. 2009 — Transfer to Real Madrid
  4. 2016 — Won UEFA Euro with Portugal
  5. 2018 — Joined Juventus
  6. 2023 — Joined Al Nassr

Key Achievements

  • 5× Ballon d'Or winner
  • 5× UEFA Champions League winner
  • All-time top scorer in Champions League
  • UEFA Euro 2016 champion with Portugal
